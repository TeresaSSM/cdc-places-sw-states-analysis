# Chronic Disease Burden and Preventive Care Access in the American Southwest

An R-based analysis evaluating chronic disease burden (diabetes, high blood pressure, 
obesity) and preventive care access (insurance coverage, routine checkups) across 437 
counties in five southwestern U.S. states (California, Arizona, New Mexico, Oklahoma, 
and Texas) using 2023 CDC PLACES County Data. 

## Key Findings

Uninsured rate showed a strong, consistent positive relationship with diabetes prevalence 
across all five states. Oklahoma has region's highest overall disease 
burden. Rural counties showed higher uninsured rates and 
higher chronic disease burden than urban counties in most states, with the widest gaps in 
New Mexico and Texas. 

## Tools

R, R Markdown, tidyverse, rstatix, ggpubr, gt
